Each is fetched at 336×336 (`w=336&h=336&fit=crop&q=80`) — 6× the 56px tile, so
it stays sharp on a 3× display.

**Worn on a model, not flat-lay.** Every category names its audience
("Women's T-Shirts", "Boy's Casual Shirts"), and at 56px a garment on a person
says who it is for far faster than the garment alone does. The seven were also
picked for distinct dominant colours — red, light blue, pink, rust, white, blue
check, teal — so no two tiles in a row read as the same swatch.

**Known caveat:** `boys-casual-t-shirts.jpg` has a Levi's wordmark on the tee.
It is unreadable at 56px and incidental brand presence is fine under the
Unsplash licence, but it does sit oddly beside a fictional brand list. Swap it
if that bothers anyone.
